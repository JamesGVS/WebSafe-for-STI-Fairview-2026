02 / THREATS
Stay Aware

Understanding
Online Threats

Cybercriminals engineer convincing traps. Knowing the threat types is your first line of defense.

01

Phishing Links

Fake pages that perfectly mimic banks, social media, and government sites to steal your login credentials and personal data.

02

QR Code Scams

Printed or digital QR codes that embed harmful links — often placed on posters, menus, or fake payment terminals.

03

Malicious Files

Disguised downloads — PDFs, executables, Office files — that contain hidden scripts to install ransomware or keyloggers.

Why Verification Matters

WebSafe gives you visibility before interaction — detect suspicious links, non-secure domains, harmful QR codes, and dangerous downloads before damage is done.

07 / RESPONSE

Stay One Step Ahead of Scammers Through Better Digital Safety

Spotted a threat? Don't panic — take these steps immediately to protect yourself and report the incident to the right authorities.

Report To Your Service Provider

Contact your ISP or mobile network immediately. Globe: Call 211 or use GlobeOne app. Smart: Call 1511 or use the Smart app.

Report To Local Authorities

PNP Cybercrime Division. Hotline: (+632) 724-3660. Email: onlinecrime.pnp@gmail.com

Notify Affected Institutions

If bank details or financial info were exposed, contact your bank immediately to freeze accounts, change credentials, and request a fraud investigation.

Do Not Interact Further

Stop clicking links, downloading files, or responding to messages from the suspected source. Disconnect from the site and close the browser tab immediately.

Report to DICT / Anti-Cybercrime

Department of Information and Communications Technology. Email: onlinecims.ocs@gmail.com. Or visit the DICT website to file an online complaint.

Preserve Evidence

Screenshot every relevant communication — the original message, the suspicious URL, and any site you visited — before deleting anything. Evidence strengthens reports.

Emergency Cybercrime Contacts

If you believe you are an active victim of cybercrime, contact authorities immediately — don't wait.
