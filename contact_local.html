01 Law Enforcement For active scams & crimes

PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group

Philippine National Police

Handles active cybercrime incidents including hacked social media accounts, online impersonation, real-time scams, online threats, and cyber harassment. File a complaint if you are actively being victimized or have evidence of an ongoing cybercrime operation. Walk-in reports are also accepted at Camp Crame, Quezon City.

02 Financial For fraud & unauthorized transactions

Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas

BSP — Central Bank of the Philippines

Regulates all banks, e-wallets (GCash, Maya, etc.), and financial institutions in the Philippines. Report unauthorized transactions, suspicious bank activity, OTP-based fraud, or any financial service that fails to address your complaint. BSP can compel banks to investigate and potentially reverse fraudulent transactions.

Unauthorized Transactions E-Wallet Fraud Bank Scams OTP Theft
8708-7087Call
consumeraffairs@bsp.gov.phEmail

Securities & Exchange Commission

SEC — Corporate Regulator

Regulates investment activities and securities markets in the Philippines. Report investment scams, crypto fraud, Ponzi or pyramid schemes, fake trading platforms, and unregistered entities soliciting investments. The SEC can issue advisories, freeze assets, and revoke business licenses of fraudulent companies.

03 Privacy For data leaks & personal info misuse

National Privacy Commission

NPC — Data Protection Authority

Enforces the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and protects citizens against unlawful collection, misuse, and exposure of personal information. Report phishing forms harvesting your data, companies that leaked your info, unauthorized sharing of personal records, or identity misuse by apps and online platforms.

04 Cyber For phishing links & fake websites

Dept. of Information & Communications Technology

DICT — National ICT Agency

The primary government body responsible for ICT policy and cybersecurity awareness in the Philippines. Report phishing websites, fake government portals, suspicious online forms mimicking official pages, and malicious links spreading via email or social media. DICT coordinates with other agencies to take down fraudulent online properties.

05 SMS / Telco For spam & phishing text messages

Globe Telecom

Globe — Telco Provider

Globe's anti-spam and fraud prevention team handles reports of unsolicited SMS messages, phishing texts impersonating banks or government agencies, OTP interception attempts, and suspicious text messages with malicious links. You can call 211 from a Globe number or forward the suspicious message directly via email for faster processing.

Smart Communications

Smart / PLDT — Telco Provider

Smart's cybersecurity and customer protection team addresses scam text messages, phishing SMS from numbers pretending to be banks or government offices, suspicious promotional messages, and SIM-swapping attempts. Dial *888 from a Smart number for immediate hotline assistance, or forward suspicious messages via email with sender number included.
